Special Admission for Overseas Koreans

A quick English guide to the 3-year and 12-year tracks for Korean nationals educated abroad.
Korean universities run a special admission for Korean nationals educated abroad (재외국민 특별전형), separate from the regular quota. Eligibility follows the KCUE Basic Rules for University Admissions and each university's admission guide (모집요강). Most tools on this site are in Korean; this page summarizes the essentials in English.
The two tracks
3-year track (3년 특례)12-year track (12년 특례)
SeatsOutside the regular quota, up to 2% of the entrance quotaOutside the regular quota, no limit
SchoolingAt least 3 years of middle/high school abroad, including at least 1 year of high schoolAll 12 years (elementary to high school) abroad
Student stayAbroad for at least 3/4 of every school yearVaries by university
ParentsBoth parents abroad for at least 2/3 of every school yearNo parent requirement
If the student and both parents are foreign nationals, apply through the separate foreign-student track (외국인 전형).
2027 admission calendar
  • Applications: July 6–10, 2026 (common period set by KCUE; each university closes within it)
  • Interviews / written tests: August 8–29, 2026 (e.g. Kyung Hee Aug 8, Korea Univ. Aug 14, SKKU Aug 21, Yonsei Aug 29)
  • First results: September 4–11, 2026 · Registration: December · Entry: March 2027
  • The 2028 schedule will be added once the KCUE rules and university guides are published.
How universities select (2027 examples)
  • Seoul National University: 12-year track only (Global Talent Admission II), document review
  • Yonsei, Korea University (3-year): stage 1 documents (3× the seats) → stage 2: stage-1 score 70% + interview 30%
  • Sogang: documents 100% · SKKU, Chung-Ang: documents 100% for most majors
  • Hanyang (3-year): written test 100% (Korean for humanities, math for sciences)
Always confirm details in each university's official admission guide — see the library for links.
Documents
  • School certificates and transcripts from every school year abroad, with an apostille (or consular confirmation in non-apostille countries)
  • Notarized Korean translations of documents not in Korean
  • Entry/exit records (출입국사실증명서) for the student and both parents, family relation certificate, parents' overseas employment proof (3-year track)
